v6.1.12 · Phase A holdRatio shadow (LOG ONLY, sim and ranking untouched) — every scan appends a shadow re-scoring block: the same qualifying candidate list the live ranking scored, re-ranked at hold horizons H ∈ {∞, 30, 14, 7, 6, 3} days with score_H = (netCredit·capture/SPY)·(365/min(H,DTE))·min(1,DTE/anchor), capture = 1−√(1−min(H,DTE)/DTE). H=∞ collapses algebraically to the live formula (capture=1, horizon=DTE) and is the always-logged identity benchmark — a P1 verdict line asserts it matches the live ranking exactly on every scan. WHY H-DAYS AND NOT h-RATIO (the original S34 spec, killed at the step-0 algebra gate before any code): the ratio transform scored credit·capture(h) annualized over h·DTE with capture(h)=1−√(1−h). But capture(h) is candidate-INDEPENDENT, so score_h = score_live · [(1−√(1−h))/h] — every candidate multiplied by the same constant. Rankings provably identical at every h; the shadow would have logged five copies of the same list forever and predictions P2–P5 were unfalsifiable by construction. The physics the ratio missed: the position holds ~6 DAYS regardless of DTE (S29), an absolute time, not a proportional one. In H-days form capture depends on H/DTE per candidate — 6 days is 100%% of a weekly, 4%% of a LEAP — so rankings genuinely diverge. Notable H=6 limit: for DTE ≤ anchor, score ∝ netCredit alone (max credit wins, subject to Δ-filter); above anchor, ∝ netCredit/DTE. Pre-registered: P1 identity null · P2 top-pick DTE falls monotonically with H, ≤~54d at H=6 · P3 Nov/Dec/Jan inversion between H=30 and H=14 · P4 at H=6 ≥1 top-5 sits bottom-half of H=∞ · P5 H=6 picks imply ~2–3 rolls/wk. Cert: scan log diff vs 0638 baseline — shadow block is the only new content, P1 verdict ✓.
